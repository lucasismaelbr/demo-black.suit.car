Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our private chauffeur services in the United States.

Unlike rideshare apps with random drivers and fluctuating surge rates, Black Suit Car is a dedicated, white-glove private chauffeur service. You receive a guaranteed luxury black SUV or sedan, a vetted professional driver in executive attire, flight tracking with complimentary wait time, and fixed upfront pricing with zero cancellations.

We operate exclusively within the United States, providing private airport transfers, city-to-city rides, corporate travel, and hourly chauffeur services in major metropolitan areas.

We monitor your flight status in real time. If your flight is delayed or arrives early, your chauffeur automatically adjusts the pickup schedule. For airport transfers, we include complimentary wait time and meet you at the designated terminal pickup area.

Our standard operating hours are 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM Eastern Time (ET). Transportation services outside these hours (late night or early dawn) are available upon advance reservation and prior approval.

Our pricing is transparent and all-inclusive. You will receive a fixed rate before confirming your ride, with no surge pricing, hidden taxes, or surprise fees.

Our fleet consists exclusively of late-model luxury Black SUVs (e.g. Cadillac Escalade, Lincoln Navigator, Mercedes-Benz) and executive sedans, featuring premium leather seating, climate control, privacy glass, Wi-Fi, and refreshments.

You can cancel or reschedule your ride with advance notice. Please contact our team as early as possible to adjust your booking without penalty.
