Our standard daily operating hours are from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM Eastern Time (ET). Reservation requests submitted through our website are reviewed between 7:00 AM and 9:00 PM ET. Black Suit Car operates on a transparent, fixed upfront pricing model. Rates are calculated based on route distance, vehicle class, estimated time, and any requested extra stops.
